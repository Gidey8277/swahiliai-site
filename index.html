Shwahili AI

Shwahili AI is an AI-powered voice assistant designed to provide natural conversations and assistance in Kiswahili, with support for Kenyan Sheng.

The app allows users to interact with an AI assistant using voice and other supported features to get information, assistance, and AI-generated responses.
